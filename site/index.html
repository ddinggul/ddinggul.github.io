On my mind 연구 분야

Three threads I keep pulling. 주요 관심사

Can AI perceive the melody of speech?

Prosody, stress, intonation — these are the features that make the same words mean completely different things. I want to know whether AI systems can learn to hear them the way a human listener does, not just pattern-match on waveforms.

What separates native from learner speech?

The gap between L1 and L2 speakers lives largely in the suprasegmentals — the rhythm, the melody, the weight of syllables. I use mel-spectrograms, TextGrid alignment, and contrastive learning to make those differences visible.

What should AI actually do for language learners?

Not just score them. I'm building pipelines for grammar correction and narrative modelling, and thinking about vocabulary systems that respect how people actually learn — not just how benchmark datasets are structured.
